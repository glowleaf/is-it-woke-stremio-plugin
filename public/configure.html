4. Move it above Cinemeta

Stremio shows the first meta addon in your install order, so this addon has to sit above Cinemeta or its page wins and the chips never appear. Stremio's UI can't reorder addons, but its API can. This logs in to api.strem.io from your browser and puts this addon (with the options above) first.

Scores are editorial / AI estimates from isitwokeornot.com (titles from 2016 onward) and isitwoke.io. This addon is not affiliated with either site or with Stremio.

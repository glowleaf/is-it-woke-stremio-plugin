The story

Paul Atreides arrives on Arrakis after his father accepts the stewardship of the dangerous planet. However, chaos ensues after a betrayal as forces clash to control melange, a precious resource.

What is woke about it

Dune builds its heroism around House Atreides, whose court is visibly multi-ethnic and humane, against the pale, brutal Harkonnens and a decadent Emperor, so virtue is coded through culture rather than lecture. The Bene Gesserit are the galaxy's real power brokers, all-female and manipulative, yet the film frames them as sinister, not admirable. Paul is the chosen figure, trained by his mother Jessica and by Duncan and Gurney, and his rise depends on their skill and sacrifice. Chani appears in visions as a romantic ideal, not a rival. The ending leaves Paul and Jessica alone among the Fremen, survival earned through his choices.Our analysis, written from the synopsis and the published scores

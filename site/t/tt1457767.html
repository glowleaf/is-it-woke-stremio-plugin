The story

Paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren work to help a family terrorized by a dark presence in their farmhouse.

What is woke about it

Ed Warren leads the exorcism prayers and physically restrains the possessed Carolyn, while Lorraine's clairvoyance supplies the crucial knowledge, as when she reads the cellar's history and sees the knitting needles in blood. Carolyn Perron fights the entity possessing her to save her daughter Andrea, and the family's reunion is the emotional payoff. The Perrons are a conventional 1970s household, and the Warrens a devout married pair whose faith and partnership are the film's moral center.

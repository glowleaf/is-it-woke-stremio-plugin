The story

After the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War (2018), the universe is in ruins. With the help of remaining allies, the Avengers assemble once more in order to reverse Thanos' actions and restore balance to the universe.

What is woke about it

The screenplay is built on male heroism and its costs: Clint loses his family in the opening, Tony records a farewell to Pepper and later dies for the universe, Steve hands Sam the shield and then takes the dance with Peggy he was owed since 1945. Women are present and capable — Natasha leads the search for Clint, Nebula beats Tony at table football, Laura and Lila anchor the Barton home — but none is written as the point of the scene. The one overtly ideological beat is the female-heroes formation in the final battle, which reads as a staged gesture rather than the film's argument. The ending is a wedding ring and a dance, not a lecture.Our analysis, written from the synopsis and the published scores

The story

Still reeling from the loss of Gamora, Peter Quill rallies his team to defend the universe and one of their own - a mission that could mean the end of the Guardians if not successful.

What is woke about it

James Gunn keeps the focus on Rocket's origin, a caged raccoon subjected to the High Evolutionary's experiments, and the film's moral engine is the rejection of that eugenic perfectionism. The villain's utopia built on culling the imperfect is condemned through Quill's crew of misfits, and the emotional payoff is Rocket reclaiming his name and leading the team. Gamora returns as a harder, memory-stripped variant whose competence is matched by Quill, Drax, Nebula and Groot; the men are foolish but never helpless, and the ending hands command to Rocket and the guardianship to Quill's chosen family.Our analysis, written from the synopsis and the published scores

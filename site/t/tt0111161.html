The story

After a banker is sentenced to life in Shawshank Prison, he forms an unlikely friendship with a seasoned inmate and clings to hope amid cruelty and corruption.

What is woke about it

Andy Dufresne is convicted of murdering his wife and her lover, and the film opens on their drunken, rough coupling before cutting to Andy in his car with a .38, loading bullets clumsily and watching the bungalow. Prison life is brutal: the Sisters assault him, and Hadley beats Bogs to death for it, dumping him off a tier onto a mop cart. Andy's decency shows in the library, the tax advice, the opera over the loudspeaker, and the tunnel he digs for nineteen years.

Methodology

Independent scorers disagree, sometimes by 60 points on the same film. This index keeps every score visible, adds one weighted average, and writes the analysis the individual scorers do not: what the themes are, in one place.

Normalisation

Sources publishing 0 to 10 are multiplied by ten; Woke Movie Score publishes 0 to 100; Virtue Vigil publishes out of 50 and is doubled. Nothing else is changed, no curve fitting, no rescaling to force agreement.

Theme scores

Five themes come from a published rubric (Identity Politics, Oikophobia, Feminism, LGBTQ+, Anti-Theism) rescaled to 0 to 100. Bossbabes and Emasculation are judged by AI from the synopsis and the scorers' own notes, because no source publishes those two as fields and titles without a rubric page would otherwise have nothing to show. The judgement is stated as ours, not attributed to a source.

What this is not

Not a review, and not a verdict on whether you should watch something. Where only one source covers a title no aggregate is shown, because averaging one number just repeats it.
